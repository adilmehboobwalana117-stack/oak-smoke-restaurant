## 1. Overview
Oak & Smoke ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers, delivery riders, and restaurant staff. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Oak & Smoke Android application, how it is used, how it is protected, and how you can delete your account and personal data at any time.

Guest customers can browse our digital menu, daily specials, and dining seating areas without signing in or submitting personal data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum data required to fulfill Cash on Delivery (COD) food orders, dining table reservations, loyalty rewards, and restaurant operations:

1. **Account & Profile Information (when you sign in with Google):**
   - Full name, email address, and Google account identifier via Firebase Authentication.
   - Optional phone number and default Hasilpur delivery address that you provide in your profile.
2. **Order, Reservation & Feedback Records:**
   - Dish selections, Cash on Delivery totals in Pakistani Rupees (PKR), kitchen notes, table reservation details (date, time slot, party size, seating zone), loyalty point balances, and submitted reviews.
3. **Foreground Device Location (only when placing a COD delivery order):**
   - With your explicit runtime permission (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`), we capture your current GPS coordinates and address label solely at the moment you tap **"Attach My Current Location"** during COD checkout so our Oak & Smoke Rider can navigate accurately to your doorstep.
   - **We never track your location in the background.**
4. **Notification Preferences (optional):**
   - With your permission on Android 13+ (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`), we display local alerts and sound notifications for order status updates, order-ready rider dispatch, table reservation confirmations, and daily specials.
5. **Authorized Staff & Rider Operations Data (Staff/Owner Portal only):**
   - Staff/Rider IDs, role tier, hashed passcodes (SHA-256), work shift schedules, payroll status, and order fulfillment counts for authorized Oak & Smoke team members.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- Process and deliver Cash on Delivery (COD) food orders in Hasilpur.
- Share your attached delivery GPS pin, address, and contact phone number with the assigned Oak & Smoke delivery rider for order delivery.
- Confirm and manage dining table reservations across Charcoal Hall, Family Cabin, and Outdoor Lawn.
- Credit and redeem Oak & Smoke Loyalty Points.
- Send order-status, rider-ready, and restaurant special alerts.
- Enable restaurant owners and managers to monitor daily sales, expenses, kitchen inventory, and staff operations.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services
We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal data to third parties, and we **do not serve third-party behavioral advertising**.

We rely on the following trusted Google infrastructure services to operate the app securely:
- **Google Firebase Authentication** (Google Sign-In via Android Credential Manager)
- **Google Cloud Firestore** (encrypted cloud database storage and real-time sync with strict role-based security rules)
- **Google Play Services Location** (foreground-only GPS capture when explicitly triggered by the user)

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS.

## 5. Permissions Summary
- **Internet & Network State:** Required to sync menus, COD orders, reservations, and offline cache recovery.
- **Foreground Location (Fine / Coarse):** Requested only when you choose to attach your delivery GPS location for COD orders. Optional for browsing the menu or reserving a table.
- **Notifications & Vibration:** Optional; used for real-time order updates and order-ready alerts.
- **No Camera, Contacts, Microphone, SMS, or Broad Storage Permissions** are requested.

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain your account profile and order records only while your account remains active or as needed to fulfill orders and restaurant accounting obligations.

**How to Delete Your Account and Personal Data:**
1. **Directly Inside the App:** Open **Profile → Privacy Policy & Data Safety → Delete My Account & Data** and confirm deletion. This immediately deletes your user profile, staff role grant (if any), and signs you out.
2. **By Email Request:** Email **adilmehboobwalana117@gmail.com** from your registered Google email with the subject *"Account Deletion Request — Oak & Smoke"*. Requests are fulfilled within **7 business days**.
