Summary: Guest customers can browse the menu and seating areas without signing in. We collect only the information needed to fulfill Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, table reservations, and loyalty points. We never track location in the background, never show ads, and never sell personal data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account & Profile: Name, email address, and Google account ID via Firebase Authentication when you sign in with Google, plus optional phone number and Hasilpur delivery address.
  • Orders, Reservations & Loyalty: Order items, PKR COD totals, kitchen notes, table bookings, customer reviews, and loyalty point balances.
  • Foreground Delivery Location: Captured only when you tap "Attach My Current Location" at COD checkout so our Rider can deliver to your exact location. No background location tracking is used.
  • Notifications: Optional Android notifications and sound alerts for order status updates, rider dispatch, and daily specials.

2. How Data Is Used & Protected

Data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore with strict role-based access rules and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Delivery location pins and contact phone numbers are visible only to the customer who placed the order and authorized Oak & Smoke staff/riders fulfilling that delivery.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In)
  • Google Cloud Firestore (real-time database & offline caching)
  • Google Play Services Location (foreground-only GPS capture)

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and profile data directly inside the app via Profile → Privacy Policy & Data Safety → Delete My Account & Data, or by emailing adilmehboobwalana117@gmail.com with your registered Google email. All deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

Oak & Smoke is intended for general restaurant customers and staff and is not directed to children under 13.
